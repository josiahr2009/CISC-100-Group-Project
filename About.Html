Meet Our Team

Behind every great meal is a passionate team. At Sand & Sea, our staff works together to ensure every guest feels welcome and well cared for.

Marcus Bennett -Head Chef

With over 15 years of experience in Caribbean cuisine, Chef Marcus brings creativity and tradition together in every dish. His passion for fresh seafood and local ingredients shapes the heart of the Sand & Sea menu.

Alisha James - Restaurant Manager

Alisha oversees daily operations and ensures that every guest enjoys a smooth and memorable experience. Known for her warm personality and attention to detail, she's often the first friendly face guests meet.

Daniel Lewis - Sous Chef

Daniel supports the kitchen team with precision and consistency. His focus on quality and presentation helps bring Chef Marcus's vision to life.
