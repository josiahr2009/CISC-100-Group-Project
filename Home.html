Main Content

Nestled along Antigua's sun-kissed coastline, Sand & Sea Restaurant offers a relaxed dining experience inspired by the colors, flavors, and rhythms of the Caribbean. With sweeping views of turquoise waters and soft white sand just steps away, the restaurant blends casual beachfront charm with thoughtful island cuisine.

Whether you're a visitor discovering Antigua for the first time or a local looking for a favorite seaside escape, Sand & Sea Restaurant invites you to slow down, savor the moment, and enjoy authentic island dining where the land meets the sea.
